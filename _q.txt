Purpose: This questionnaire is designed for quarterly or baseline facility assessment of readiness to provide safe maternal and newborn services under Domain 3: Facility Readiness and Safe Systems. Assessors should combine interview responses with direct observation, register review, LMIS/KHIS verification, and facility walk-through findings.

Print-use instructions: Print one copy per facility assessment. Complete the facility details before starting. Scoring guide: Yes = 2 points; Partial = 1 point; No = 0 points; Not applicable = excluded from denominator. Domain score = total points achieved divided by total possible points, expressed as a percentage. Facilities scoring 80% and above may be considered ready, 60–79% partially ready, and below 60% not ready and requiring priority corrective action.
